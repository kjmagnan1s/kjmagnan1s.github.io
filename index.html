About

I believe that if we get AI right for public safety, we get it right for all of public sector.

I started my career as a public servant, working on the ground in communities facing real challenges. That perspective shaped everything that followed: research at the University of Chicago, and now leading AI strategy for public sector clients at Slalom.

Today, I'm a Principal Consultant co-leading Slalom's Justice and Public Safety practice. I help government agencies navigate the complexities of technology and AI adoption: not just the technology, but the policy, ethics, and change management that determine whether it actually works.
