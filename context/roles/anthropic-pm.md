## The Role in Context

This is a Product Operations Manager role on Anthropic's Growth, Enterprise, and Verticals team, specifically focused on Public Sector. The role bridges strategy and execution - working with Product Managers and Engineers to identify bottlenecks, streamline workflows, and scale impact. It's hands-on tactical execution balanced with strategic initiatives.

Key responsibilities fall into three buckets:
1. **Inputs to Product Teams** - Voice of customer synthesis, feedback routing, metrics/dashboards, stakeholder engagement
2. **Ops of the Product Org** - Team rhythms, rituals, operational models, cross-functional collaboration
3. **Outputs from Product Teams** - Early Access Programs, launch motions, roadmap visibility, internal communication

## Company Context

Anthropic is the safety-focused AI lab behind Claude. Founded by former OpenAI researchers, they're building AI that's reliable, interpretable, and steerable. The company has raised significant funding and is one of the leading frontier AI companies.

What makes Anthropic different:
- **Mission-driven**: Not just building AI, but building AI that's safe and beneficial
- **Research-first**: They publish their safety research and share findings
- **Constitutional AI**: Their approach to alignment through AI self-supervision
- **Public benefit corporation**: Legal structure reinforces mission over pure profit

The Public Sector team is newer but strategic. Government adoption of frontier AI is high-stakes - the consequences of getting it wrong are measured in public trust and civil liberties. Anthropic needs people who understand both the opportunity and the responsibility.
