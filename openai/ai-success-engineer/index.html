Motivation to Apply

I started on patrol at St. Louis County PD, moved to the University of Chicago Crime Lab to research what actually changes outcomes in public safety, and now lead Slalom's $50M Justice and Public Safety practice. The thread across all of it has been the same: governments carry decades of tech debt that residents pay for in worse services, longer waits, and missed outcomes. AI is the first tool I have worked with that lets agencies leapfrog that debt instead of refactoring it for another generation, and I have spent the last three years proving that out inside the highest-stakes corners of state and local government. I want to bring that work to OpenAI's customers because lasting effectiveness for the residents on the other side of every government desk is the only measure that has ever mattered to me.
